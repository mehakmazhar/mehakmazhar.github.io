About

AI Researcher and Educator with an MS in Artificial Intelligence from NED University and over five years of experience across Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, LLMs, and Generative AI.

My focus sits at the intersection of multimodal AI, text generation, and diffusion-based image generation — with a strong pull toward research, emerging AI technologies, and building things that hold up outside a notebook. I currently lecture at FAST-NUCES Karachi and run SkillVention, an AI/ML training initiative I founded in 2021.

My path here started in Biomedical Engineering — six years working on ECG/EEG systems and hospital equipment before I moved fully into AI. That background still shapes how I think about applying models to real, high-stakes problems.
