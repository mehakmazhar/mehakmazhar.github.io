About Me

Turning research into real-world impact

I'm an AI Researcher and Educator with an MS in Artificial Intelligence (NED University, CGPA 3.60) and 5+ years of experience across Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, NLP, LLMs and Generative AI.

My work focuses on multimodal AI, text generation, and diffusion-based image generation (Stable Diffusion, SDXL, PixArt-Sigma, FLUX.1). I started in Biomedical Engineering, which gave me a strong foundation in signals, healthcare data and hardware, and moved into AI to build systems that matter.

As a Lecturer at FAST-NUCES and founder of SkillVention, I mentor students on applied AI projects and theses, and I regularly speak at tech communities about emerging AI trends.
